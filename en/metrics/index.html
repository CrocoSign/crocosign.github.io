What the ensembles are made of

MS: six Uni-Sign models chosen one by one on the validation set. They are the OpenASL and How2Sign transfer models, each with two random seeds, a model trained on OpenASL, then How2Sign, then PJM, and a model trained on a mix of the references and translations by other models that had not seen those clips.

SI: two Uni-Sign models, with OpenASL and How2Sign transfer.

An ensemble averages the models' predictions at every next word, so it is not a single-model result, and it costs as much computation as it has models.
